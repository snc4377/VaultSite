|**Function**|**Reads content**|**Executes**|**Remote URL**|
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|**PHP**||||
|`include()`/`include_once()`|✅|✅|✅|
|`require()`/`require_once()`|✅|✅|❌|
|`file_get_contents()`|✅|❌|✅|
|`fopen()`/`file()`|✅|❌|❌|
|**NodeJS**||||
|`fs.readFile()`|✅|❌|❌|
|`fs.sendFile()`|✅|❌|❌|
|`res.render()`|✅|✅|❌|
|**Java**||||
|`include`|✅|❌|❌|
|`import`|✅|✅|✅|
|**.NET**||||
|`@Html.Partial()`|✅|❌|❌|
|`@Html.RemotePartial()`|✅|❌|✅|
|`Response.WriteFile()`|✅|❌|❌|
|`include`|✅|✅|✅|
